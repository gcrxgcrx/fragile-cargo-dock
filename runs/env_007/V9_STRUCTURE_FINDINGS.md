# V9 FINDINGS — giving the generator the reward's *structure* produces the first LLM reward that matches the native one

Pre-registered in `runs/env_007/V9_STRUCTURE_PREREGISTRATION.md` (hypothesis, protocol,
predictions V9-1..V9-4 and verdict rules fixed before generation). Prompt
`prompts/eureka_01_initial_reward_v9.md`, generated by `make_prompt_v9.py` on top of v8 (diff in
`runs/env_007/v9_prompt.diff`). Raw: `runs/env_007/prompt_ladder_v9/`.

block **38000-38059**:

| candidate | success | dock | mean native return | max consecutive stable steps |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **`cand_02`** | **58/60 = 96.7 %** | **0.98** | **299.44** | (see below) |
| `cand_06` | 0/60 | 0.00 | −0.70 | 0.00 |

Reference points on the same block:

| reward | success | note |
|---|---:|---|
| native reward through the wrapper (4 seeds) | 47 / 52 / 54 / 56 | the environment's own reward |
| hand-written `probeD` | 45/60 | previous best observation-only arm |
| hand-written `control_v1` (4 seeds) | 0 / 35 / 43 / 54 | the project's control |
| best unedited LLM candidate before v9 (v8 `cand_01`) | **4/60** | |
| every other LLM candidate ever produced | 0–5/60 | |

`cand_02`'s mean native return is **299.44** against the native reward's canonical standard
score of **299.65**: it is at the environment's ceiling, and it is an LLM-written reward that
uses only `obs` and `action`.

## 2. The mechanism, and why the two candidates differ so much

`cand_02` (trained) realised reward composition:

| component | magnitude share | per-episode sum |
|---|---:|---:|
| `terminal_success` (+300 one-off) | **0.893** | 285.0 |
| `settled_bonus` (per-step hold) | 0.070 | 22.5 |
| `dock_enter` (+5) | 0.016 | 5.0 |
| `progress` (signed, per metre) | 0.013 | 4.06 |
| `approach_cargo` | 0.005 | 1.22 |
| `roughness` proxy | 0.002 | −0.60 |

The **native** reward's terminal term is 299.8 of a 308.4 total, i.e. ~96 % of the return; the
v9 candidate is at **89 %**. In-training self-evaluation: 19 of 20 episodes terminate in success.

`cand_06` (failed) is the mirror image and the reason V9-4 was written down in advance: its
reward is **87 % boundary guard** (per-episode −12.8) with **`progress` and `terminal_success`
both at activation 0.000** — the policy never moves the crate at all, and delivery is 0/60.

So the family is bimodal: with the same stated structure, one candidate reproduces the native
reward's economics and reaches 96.7 %, another collapses to immobility. **What separates them is
the relative weight of the penalty terms, not the presence of the terms.** V9-4 ("at most 4/8
above 20/60") is consistent with what can be seen: 1 of the 2 trained candidates succeeded, and
the 89 %-versus-87 % composition split shows how small that margin is.

**family: n = 8, mean 18.0/60 (30 %), sd 25.2, three candidates >= 20/60.**

This is the distribution the pipeline will actually draw from, and it is strongly bimodal: two
candidates at the native reward's level, one mid, five immobile. V9-4 (pre-registered as "at most
4/8 above 20/60") is met at 3/8.

Two consequences, both quantitative:

* **Per-draw success probability for this prompt is ~3/8 = 37 %** (>= 20/60). A 4-candidate
  generation therefore has a ~85 % chance of containing at least one such candidate, and the
  pipeline's real problem is *not* finding one but keeping it (§2b: single-seed selection).
* **The mean family performance is 30 %**, i.e. a pipeline that reports a mean over its runs is
  reporting "roughly one candidate in three works", not "the method reaches 96 %".

## 2b. The seed replication: the ceiling came with the information, the *stability* did not

`cand_02` was retrained at three training seeds (1.2 M each, same block 38000-38059):

| reward | seed 0 | seed 1 | seed 2 | mean | **sd** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **native reward** (passthrough, seeds 0-3) | 52 | 47 | 56 | 54 | 52.3 | **3.9** |
| **v9 `cand_02`** | **58** | **35** | **0** | — | 31.0 | **29.2** |
| hand-written `C0`/`C1` (4 + 3 seeds) | 0 / 45 / 0 | 35 / 21 / 13 | 43 / — / — | 54 / — / — | 26.4 | 20.9 |

The failing seeds are not a scoring artefact — they are immobility, and the mechanism is legible
in the trained component table:

| run | `progress` active | `progress` ep-sum | `approach_cargo` active | **`terminal_success` active** | dominant term |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| seed 0 (58/60) | 0.708 | 4.06 | 0.982 | **0.00389** | `terminal_success` 0.89 |
| seed 1 (35/60) | 0.809 | 3.99 | 0.992 | **0.00249** | `terminal_success` 0.76 |
| seed 2 (0/60) | 0.466 | 2.12 | 0.938 | **0.00000** | `approach_cargo` 0.43 |

The candidate's coefficients are faithful to the recipe (`approach_cargo` +1.0/m, `progress`
+1.0/m, `dock_enter` +5, `action_cost` −0.0005, `time_cost` −0.002), with three deviations of its
own choosing: a 25x stronger roughness (−0.5 vs −0.02), an added `settled_bonus` of +2/step (the
v8 rule it inherited), and **no `terminal_failure` term at all**. It reaches 96.7 % when the
policy happens to reach the dock, and never moves at all when it does not.

**So the answer to "does giving the source let it succeed?" is layered:**

1. **Structure is the wall.** 4/60 (v8) -> 58/60 (v9) from information alone; the reward's
   internal composition now matches the native one (terminal term 89 % vs native 96 %).
2. **Stability is not in the structure.** With nigh-identical structure to the native reward,
   the v9 candidate still spans 0-58/60 across seeds. The native reward spans 47-56/60. What the
   native reward has and this candidate lacks is not yet identified; the three candidate
   differences listed above (roughness 25x, the extra settled bonus, the missing failure penalty)
   are the concrete things to ablate, one at a time, against this candidate.
3. Therefore the earlier session conclusion — "the binding constraint is optimisation variance,
   not the reward text" — is **half right**: it is not the *text* that decides the ceiling (the
   text moved the ceiling from 4/60 to 58/60), but the *text as written* still does not buy the
   native reward's seed stability. The two questions are separate and both are now measurable.

## 2c. The resolution: one candidate reproduces the native reward's *stability*, another does not (22:12)

Three training seeds per candidate, all on block 38000-38059 (`run_v9_seeds.py`, raw
`runs/env_007/prompt_ladder_v9/eval_top2_seeds.json`):

| reward | seed 0 | seed 1 | seed 2 | mean | **sd** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **v9 `cand_00`** | 55 | 54 | 52 | **53.7** | **1.5** |
| native reward through the wrapper (4 seeds) | 52 | 47 | 56 | 52.3 | 3.9 |
| v9 `cand_02` | 58 | 35 | 0 | 31.0 | 29.2 |
| hand-written `C0`/`C1` (7 seeds) | 0 / 45 / 0 | 43 / 21 / 13 | 54 / — / — | 26.4 | 20.9 |

**`cand_00` matches the native reward's mean (53.7 vs 52.3) and is at least as stable (sd 1.5
vs 3.9), with three seeds that never fall below 52/60.** It is an LLM-written reward that uses
only `obs` and `action`, and it is the first such reward in this project that is both strong and
seed-robust.

So the spread of `cand_02` is **not** a property of the prompt or of the structure: another
candidate generated from the identical prompt, with the identical term set, is stable. The
difference is in what each candidate chose for the coefficients it was free to choose —
`cand_02` deviated from the recipe in three ways (roughness 25x stronger, an added
`settled_bonus` of +2/step inherited from the v8 rule, and no `terminal_failure` term), while
`cand_00` did not. That makes the coefficient deviations, not the missing information, the
remaining object of study, and it is now a two-candidate ablation rather than a speculation.

## 3. What this establishes

1. **The information hypothesis is confirmed at the prompt level.** The gap between "LLM rewards
   score 0-5 %" and "this environment's own reward scores ~85 %" was, to a first approximation,
   that the generator was never told the reward's structure. Told the structure — nine named
   terms with their semantics and weights, and with every privileged channel still closed — it
   produced a reward that ties the native one.
2. **The structure is expressible without the privileged channel.** The only term the contract
   cannot recover is the impulse-based `roughness`; the candidate built a contact × closing-speed
   proxy for it (worth −0.60/episode, i.e. small) and still reached the ceiling. The 98.3 %
   (`probeC`, reads `info`) versus 73.3 % (`probeD`, proxy) gap did **not** bound v9 at 73 %.
3. **The claim "success is seed-dominated, not reward-dominated" must be qualified.** It holds
   for the fragile rewards (hand-written control: 0/35/43/54; ×100: 31/43/0), but the native
   reward is stable across four seeds (47-56/60). A reward with the right structure is *not* a
   lottery; the lottery was a property of the specific rewards that were being written. The
   remaining question is how much of that stability v9 candidates inherit — one seed is not
   enough to claim it, and that is the obvious next measurement (4 seeds of `cand_02`).
4. **The best-ever LLM result in this project moved from 4/60 to 58/60** on the same evaluation
   block, and it was obtained by changing *what the generator is told*, not by changing the
   search, the operator, the evidence channel, or the training budget.

## 4. What it does not establish

* **It is not a search result.** v9 is a scaffold I wrote; the candidate did not have to discover
  the structure. Any claim of the form "the method found a working reward" is still unsupported —
  what is supported is "the generator can implement a working reward when given its structure".
* **n = 2 trained candidates, one training seed each.** The family could be mostly `cand_06`-like;
  only the structure check (7/7) is at n = 8, and it is mechanical. The next experiment is fixed:
  train the rest of the family, and re-run `cand_02` at 4 training seeds on this same block.
* **The task's difficulty as a search problem is unchanged.** The information was supplied by a
  human who had read the environment source. Turning that into a method claim requires the
  pipeline to obtain the same information from the environment itself, which is exactly what the
  original setup forbade.
